Purrdr: Agent Chat

在 iPhone 與 iPad 上，看、回覆、操作你電腦上由 herdr 管理的 coding agent,也能直接開終端機進那台電腦。

  • 所有 Agent 一個列表：哪個在工作、哪個做完了、哪個在等你。
  • 在聊天裡回答 Agent 的提問與權限確認，送 prompt、slash 指令與圖片。
  • 需要時開一個完整的終端機。
  • 經 Tailscale SSH 或 OpenSSH 直接連到你的電腦：對話不經過我們的伺服器。
  • Purrdr Plus(選用訂閱):Agent 需要你時推播通知，並在你的裝置之間同步主機清單。

還沒有主機也能先用 App 內的 Demo 主機試遍每個畫面。

Purrdr: Agent Chat

Read, answer, and steer the coding agents that herdr runs on your computer — and open a terminal into it — from your iPhone or iPad.

  • Every agent in one list: which are working, which are done, and which need you.
  • Answer questions and permission prompts in the chat; send prompts, slash commands, and images.
  • Open a full terminal whenever you need one.
  • Connects straight to your computer over Tailscale SSH or OpenSSH — your conversations never pass through our servers.
  • Purrdr Plus (optional subscription): a notification when an agent needs you, and your host list synced across your devices.

No host yet? The built-in Demo host lets you try every screen first.
